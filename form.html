Scholarship information

Prepare well.
Apply with confidence.

Use this guide to understand the information commonly requested for foundation scholarship opportunities. Current application dates and final requirements will always be announced by the foundation.

On this page

Applications are not collected on this website.

This protects applicants from sending sensitive documents into an unconnected form.

01

Before applying

Confirm that an application round is officially open and read its specific eligibility conditions. Never pay anyone to access or submit a foundation scholarship application.

  • Check the announced deadline
  • Confirm your study level is eligible
  • Use an email and phone number you can access
  • Make sure every detail you provide is accurate
02

What to prepare

Requirements may change between programmes, but preparing these items early can make a future application easier.

IdentityFull name, contact details, state and local government area
EducationInstitution, course, level, matriculation details and academic record
DocumentsPassport photograph, transcript or results, and requested identification letters
Your storyA clear explanation of your goals, circumstances and how support would help
03

Request scholarship updates

If you want to ask about the next application window, contact the foundation directly. Do not attach sensitive documents until you receive an official request and submission method.
